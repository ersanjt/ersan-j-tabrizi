Structuring Production Node.js Systems

A field note on workflows, data, deployment, and maintainability.

Start with the workflow

Routes and frameworks are only the surface. Authentication, customers, documents, approvals, reports, and administration should follow clear business boundaries.

Keep records traceable

Financial and operational changes need explicit services, predictable database access, and a readable history of who changed what.

Design for the day after launch

Backups, monitoring, deployment, access control, and recovery belong in the architecture from the start.
